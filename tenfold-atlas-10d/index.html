YOUR TENFOLD ATLAS

Coordinates 1–3 are ordinary movement axes (X, Y, Z). Axes 4–10 are fictional extra spatial coordinates. The ten named realms are navigable slices of that larger space, not ten separate physical dimensions.
EXPEDITION FIELD MANUAL

How to explore

Desktop movement

W A S D to walk, Shift to sprint, Space to jump. Drag the scene (or use arrow keys) to look. Click the world then use your keyboard.

iPhone / touch

Drag the left joystick to walk. Swipe the right half of the screen to look. Use the buttons to talk, jump, and scan.

Shift dimensions

Choose an axis from the dimension panel, or press keys 1–9 and 0. On mobile, swipe the dimension strip at the top.

Discover and talk

Find three golden echoes in each realm and press F or INTERACT near them. Talk to other characters for their stories and hints.

Scan the world

Press R or SCAN to project the undiscovered echoes onto your view for a few seconds.

Your progress

Open ☷ to review the atlas. Your echoes and anchors are saved on this device automatically.
